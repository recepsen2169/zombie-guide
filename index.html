Zombie Apocalypse Survival Guide

How to survive the outbreak at the OTC Springfield Campus.

Three Survival Rules

  1. Rule #1: Cardio! keep moving and always check your exits...

  2. Rule #2: Travel Light. Only carry what you absolutely need to escape danger zones.
  3. Rule #3: Stay Quiet. Avoid making loud noises.

Essential Supplies

  • Heavy-duty backpack: To carry tools hands-free.
  • Canned food: for long-lasting nutrition from the cafeteria.
  • A flashlight: To navigate dark hallways if the power goes out.

The Safe Zone

The OTC Information Technology Center (ITC) building. it works great because it has secure badge-access doors and heavy computer desks to barricade entryways.

Pi Day Visitor Challenge

The cafeteria is surrounded by zombies. Do you try to fight your way in for fresh pizza, or do you stay safe and survive on vending machine snakcs in the library?
